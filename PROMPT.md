# The Prompt

Build a Final Fantasy VI–inspired idle RPG that runs in a mobile browser, at the
quality level of a top-grossing premium mobile game. Melvor Idle is the systems
template — skills, XP curves, mastery, offline tick replay, faucets and sinks. Take
its math, not its content. FFVI is the art and world reference — SNES pixel art,
espers, jobs, magic, opera-house melodrama — but invent every name, character, and
monster yourself. Nothing from RuneScape.

The bar is in `reference/`: a premium mobile idle UI screenshot for chrome and
polish, real FFVI screenshots for pixel fidelity, and Melvor's published XP and
economy tables for the math. Everything you build gets compared against it.

Divide the goal into the smallest pieces that can be built and judged on their own.
For each piece, fan out a builder and a separate critic with fresh context. The
critic must run the real thing, screenshot it, and compare it against the bar blind
and side by side — no labels, just pick which one shipped. Critics should be brutal.
Whatever the biggest remaining gap is, send it back and go again.

Keep a live progress page updated as the work evolves so I can watch it.

Loop until our output wins the blind comparison or I stop the run. Fan out
subagents and ultracode.
